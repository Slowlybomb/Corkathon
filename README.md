# Corkathon

A field-monitoring project for the Cork Hackathon run by UCC Computer Science Society. A team of three is exploring a OneSoil-inspired dashboard, with a custom 5-inch FPV drone carrying a Realme RMX3363 phone as the proposed camera and computing unit.

The product question: **Where should a farmer inspect first, why, and how recent is the evidence?**

The vision combines soil and vegetation coverage, unusual patches, possible wet areas, and soil-property estimates in one map-based panel. The hackathon demonstration will show the physical construction and a dashboard survey replay; **there will be no drone flight**. Recorded observations, simulated movement, and measured or estimated results must be clearly distinguished.

See the [project plan](docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md) for scope, dashboard contents, team responsibilities, delivery stages, demo story, and open questions.

Deadline: **19:00 on 7 November 2026, Cork local time**. A teammate owns choosing the analysis target and finding the dataset; that search is underway.

Status: product planning. Technology choices and implementation are intentionally deferred.
